# Client Profile

## Purpose
To document who the client is, what they need, and how success will be measured, so that every design and technical decision can be checked against real client goals rather than developer assumption.

## Overview
Moments in Blooms is a luxury event styling business operating in Melbourne, Australia, providing styling and decor services for weddings, corporate functions, and private events.

## Current Status
Client profile confirmed directly with the client during project scoping; to be revisited if the client's positioning or service offering changes materially.

## Responsibilities
**Business:** Moments in Blooms
**Location:** Melbourne, Australia
**Industry:** Luxury event styling / event decor and design

**Target Market:** Couples and event organizers in Melbourne and surrounding regions seeking premium, design-led event styling rather than budget or DIY options.

**Target Audience:** Primarily engaged couples planning weddings (18–24 month lead time typical of the industry), plus corporate event planners and private hosts booking styling for milestone events.

**Brand Voice:** Warm but refined; confident without being flashy; descriptive and sensory language around florals, textures, and atmosphere rather than generic marketing copy.

**Business Goals:**
- Convert website visitors into qualified enquiries.
- Present a portfolio that matches the in-person quality of the client's styling work.
- Reduce reliance on manual, ad-hoc communication channels (Instagram DMs, phone) for capturing leads.
- Build a foundation that can support online booking and payments in a later phase, without needing to rebuild the site.

**Pain Points:**
- No centralized place to showcase styled events at a professional standard.
- Enquiries currently scattered across social media and phone, with no shared record or notes.
- No ability to update website content (gallery, services, FAQs) without contacting a developer.
- No SEO presence to speak of, so the business is effectively invisible in organic search.

**Success Metrics:**
- Increase in qualified enquiries received through the website contact form.
- Client's ability to update gallery/services/FAQ content independently, with zero developer tickets for routine content changes.
- Faster enquiry response time due to centralized, searchable enquiry records.

## Implementation Notes
These goals directly informed the CMS scope (client needs to self-serve content changes) and the Enquiries module design (search + notes, because the client is currently managing this manually and needs structure, not just a form).
